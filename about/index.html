Where it began

Since 1996

Random Computer Solutions began in 1996 in a small office in Chennai, writing customised software in FoxPro for the DOS platform. Every business got software shaped around the way it already kept its books.

In the millennium year, the software moved to Windows as Random™ XP, a full GUI version named for the Windows XP days. It kept accounts the way an Indian accountant writes them: credit on the left, debit on the right, a two-sided cash book, and final accounts in the Indian layout, all quick to work from the keyboard.

Since then, many versions of the software have been developed and delivered to customers. Alongside the software, the company has also run a computer hardware business, with maintenance and service for its customers. After more than two decades of serving businesses, Random™ software has now been rebuilt as Random™ AI, the cloud and AI version.
