Where it began

Random XP™

Random XP™ was named in the Windows XP days, as billing and accounting software for traders and small businesses in India. It kept accounts the way an Indian accountant writes them: credit on the left, debit on the right, a two-sided cash book, and final accounts in the Indian layout.

It was built to be quick. Every screen could be worked from the keyboard, with F2 for journal, F3 for cash book, F5 for ledger and F6 for sales, so a day's entries took minutes. Each installation was licensed per computer and unlocked with an activation code, and registered customers have used it to run their books, GST bills and year-end accounts.
