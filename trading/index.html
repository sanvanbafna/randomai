Random Trading App

Find stocks, place orders with the exit already set, and see exactly what you made, by month, day, stock and broker account. In your browser, on your computer or phone.

Ask for access
The app is private. Registered users sign in with the login they were given.

What it does

Built so the plan is decided before the trade, not during it.

Scanner and watchlists

Named scan rules, a background scanner for today's qualifiers, and watchlists you can import from TradingView.

One-click orders

Market, limit and stop orders, intraday or delivery, with the stop-loss and target sent together with the entry.

Broker channels

Each broker account is a channel. Orders, positions, trades and ledger are kept per channel.

Profit and loss

Drill down by month, day, stock, type or channel, all the way to single fills, with every charge shown.

Holdings

Live holdings and valuation, and delivery sales for the year.

Companies and users

Several companies, each with its own data, and admin, trader or read-only viewer roles.
